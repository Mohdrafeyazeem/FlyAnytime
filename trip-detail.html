Day-by-Day Choreography

Detailed Itinerary Timeline

Full Escort
Day 1 • Imperial Arrival Delhi ➔ Jaipur

VIP Airport Reception & City Palace Private Chambers

Meet & greet at VIP General Aviation terminal in Delhi. 45-minute scenic charter hop to Jaipur. Private check-in at Rambagh Palace with royal vermilion blessing and champagne high tea in the peacock gardens.

Day 2 • Fortresses & Heritage Jaipur Enclave

Sunrise Amber Fort Safari & Royal Gem Curations

Early morning private jeep ascent to Amber Fort before public opening hours. Private jeweler studio viewing with royal gemologists, followed by sunset cocktails on Nahargarh Fort ramparts.

Day 3 • Romantic Waters Jaipur ➔ Udaipur

Private Flight to Lake Pichola & Taj Lake Palace Sanctuary

Direct morning flight to Udaipur Maharana Pratap Airport. Private luxury boat transfer to Taj Lake Palace floating on Lake Pichola. Evening royal sunset cruise with live Mewari sitar maestros.

Day 4 • Blue City Royalty Udaipur ➔ Jodhpur

Mehrangarh Private Access & Umaid Bhawan Gala Dinner

Short private hop over the Aravalli hills to Jodhpur. VIP curator-guided walkthrough of Mehrangarh Fort's royal armory and howdah collection. Five-course Marwari banquet at Umaid Bhawan Palace.
